## 🛠️ Action Plan — How to Land 30+ LPA

### 1. DSA Preparation (Critical for FAANG + Hedge Funds)
- Target **150+ LeetCode problems** — focus on Medium/Hard
- Key patterns: **Graphs, Trees, DP, Binary Search, Sliding Window**
- Practice in plain text editor without IDE autocomplete
- Timeline: 4-6 weeks of consistent practice

### 2. ML System Design (Critical for L4+ roles)
- Study designing these systems at scale:
  - ✅ RAG systems (you already built one — go deeper on scaling)
  - ✅ LLM serving infrastructure & optimization
  - ✅ Agentic workflow orchestration
  - ✅ Recommendation engines
  - ✅ Text-to-SQL systems at scale

### 3. Referral Strategy (3-5x more effective than cold apply)
- Use **LinkedIn** to connect with engineers at target companies
- Personalize messages: mention your specific projects (Agent Forge, Alpha Copilot)
- Attend AI meetups in Hyderabad — GenAI Hyderabad, TFUG, etc.
- Target referrals from State Street alumni who moved to these companies

### 4. Build Public Presence
- **GitHub**: Ensure projects are well-documented with READMEs, demos, architecture diagrams
- **Blog**: Write 2-3 articles on Medium/LinkedIn about:
  - Building agentic AI platforms in enterprise
  - Text-to-SQL: lessons from research to production
  - Pretraining a 1B parameter LLM: what I learned
- **Twitter/X**: Share AI insights, engage with AI community

### 5. Resume Optimization
- Quantify all impact with numbers (you already do this well ✅)
- Tailor resume slightly for different company types:
  - **AI Labs**: Emphasize pretraining, research papers, JAX/TPU
  - **Big Tech**: Emphasize production systems, scale, CI/CD, testing
  - **Fintech/Banks**: Emphasize State Street work, financial domain, compliance
  - **Startups**: Emphasize breadth, shipping speed, full-stack AI

### 6. Location Advantage 📍
> [!TIP]
> **Hyderabad is the #2 AI hub** after Bangalore with offices of Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Apple, JP Morgan, Goldman Sachs, DE Shaw, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Oracle, and more. Leverage local networking!

### 7. Salary Negotiation
- Always negotiate — never accept the first offer
- With **competing offers**, you can push **20-30% above** initial numbers
- Ask about stock/ESOP vesting schedules, signing bonuses, and relocation support
- Total compensation = Base + Bonus + Stock + Benefits — optimize for total, not just base
